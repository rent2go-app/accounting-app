Stripe → Car map

A Stripe subscription names its car in the product — “NISSAN SENTRA 2019 - BLACK” — but that text is not reliable enough to match on every sync: colours disagree between the two systems, and two subscriptions can otherwise land on the same vehicle. So each product is resolved to a car once, here, and trusted from then on. Once a product is mapped, cancelling its subscription releases the car and starting a new one claims it, automatically.
