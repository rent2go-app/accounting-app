Fleet → Owner & Billing

Each live car is tied to the Stripe account that bills it, and therefore to the customer portal its renter should be sent to. Most are set automatically from the subscription that holds the car — set the rest here. A car with no billing account will leave its renter without a working portal link.

The integrity check at the top compares what Stripe is billing against what the app believes, so a car recorded twice or a rental that quietly ended shows up rather than sitting unnoticed.
